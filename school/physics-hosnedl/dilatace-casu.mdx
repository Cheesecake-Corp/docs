$$\Delta t' =t_2'-t_1' = \Delta t_0$$ - vlastní čas trvání děje

$$
\Delta t = t_2 - t_1 = \frac{t_2' + \frac{v}{c^2} \cdot x'}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} - \frac{t_1' + \frac{v}{c^2} \cdot x'}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} = \frac{t_2' - t_1'}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}}
$$

$$
\Delta t = \frac{\Delta t'}{\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}} = \frac{\Delta t_0}{\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}}
$$

$$
\Delta t > \Delta t_0
$$
