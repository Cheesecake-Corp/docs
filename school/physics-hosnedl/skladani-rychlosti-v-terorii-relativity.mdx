- $$v = 20 \, \text{km} \cdot \text{h}^{-1}$$ - rychlost vagónu
- $$u' = 5 \, \text{km} \cdot \text{h}^{-1}$$ - rychlost uživatele vagónu ve vagónu

**Klasická fyzika:** $$u = v + u' = 20 + 5 = 25 \, \text{km} \cdot \text{h}^{-1}$$

<Accordion title="Příklady, proč to někdy nefunguje">
  - $$v = \frac{2}{3}c, u' = \frac{2}{3}c, u = \frac{4}{3}c$$
  - $$v = c, u' = c, u = 2c$$

  → too bad
</Accordion>

**Spešl fyzika:** 

<Frame>
  ![Skladani2](/images/skladani2.png "Skladani2")
</Frame>

- v $$S'$$:
  - od $$t_1'$$ do $$t_2'$$ a od $$x_1'$$ do $$x_2'$$
  - $$u' = \frac{x_2' - x_1'}{t_2' - t_1'}$$
- v $$S$$:
  - $$t_1$$... $$t_2$$, $$x_1$$... $$x_2$$
  - $$u = \frac{x_2 - x_1}{t_2 - t_1}$$
  - $$u = \frac{x_2 - x_1}{t_2 - t_1} = \frac{(x_2' + vt_2') - (x_1' + vt_1')}{(t_2' + \frac{v}{c^2}x_2')-(t_1' + \frac{v}{c^2}x_1')} = \frac{(x_2' - x_1')+v(t_2'-t_1')}{(t_2'-t_1')+\frac{v}{c^2}(x_2'-x_1')} = \frac{u' + v}{1 + \frac{u'v}{c^2}}$$

$$
\boldsymbol{u = \frac{u' + v}{1 + \frac{v}{c^2}u'}}
$$

$$v \rightarrow 0$$: jmenovatel bude blízko $$1$$, a bude platit $$u = u' + v$$

<Accordion title="Oprava těch, co nefungují">
  - $$v = \frac{2}{3}c, u' = \frac{2}{3}c, u = \frac{\frac{4}{3}c}{1 + \frac{\frac{4}{9}c^2}{c^2}} = \frac{12}{13}c$$
  - $$v = c, u' = c, u = \frac{2c}{1 + \frac{c^2}{c^2}} = c$$
</Accordion>
